# APK v2.6 Ready with Wastage Handling! 🗑️

The new APK version **2.6.0** is ready. This update addresses the request to handle "Wastage" entries separately from actual sales data.

## 🆕 New Feature: Wastage Handling
You can now create sales entries for a customer named **"Wastage"** (or "Wastge"), and these entries will no longer inflate your profit, revenue, or sales statistics.

### Key Changes:
- **Dashboard Stats**: Profit, Revenue, and Sales (Kg) metrics now exclude wastage entries.
- **Charts**: Graphs for sales history and item sales no longer include wastage.
- **Reports**: The Analysis and Statistics modules exclude wastage from general reports.
- **Inventory Accuracy**: Wastage entries still reduce product stock levels to ensure your physical inventory remains accurate.
- **Not Received**: Amounts owed by the "Wastage" customer are excluded from the "Not Received" total.

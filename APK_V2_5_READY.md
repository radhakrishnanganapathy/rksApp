# APK v2.5 Ready with Daily Reminders! 🔔

The new APK version **2.5.0** is ready. This update adds the much-requested daily reminder feature.

## 🆕 New Feature: Daily Reminders
You can now set a daily reminder to enter your business data at a time that suits you.

### How to use:
1. Go to the **More** menu (or Farm Settings / Home Menu).
2. Tap on the new **Reminders** 🔔 option.
3. Toggle "Daily Reminder" to **ON**.
4. Set your preferred time (e.g., 9:00 PM).
5. Tap **Save Settings**.
6. The app will send you a gentle, motivational reminder every day at that exact time!

### Reminder Style
The reminders are designed to be polite and motivating, such as:
*   *"Time to record your daily wins! 🌟 Entering your expenses helps you grow your business."*
*   *"Don't forget to track your progress! Small steps lead to big success. 🚀"*
